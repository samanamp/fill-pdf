Your signature

Choose a style, then click the page where it should go. You can move, resize and recolour it afterwards. Check that the recipient accepts typed signatures. If unsure, print the form and sign by hand.

Save this dynamic form

This is a dynamic (XFA) form. Its fields are saved into the form itself, but the text, ticks and signature you added on top can't be stored in this kind of form.

  • Save form only: the official fillable form with your answers. Open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader to validate and print.
  • Print to PDF: a flat copy with everything, including what you added. Choose “Save as PDF” in the print window.

Find and redact

Spaces and dashes are ignored, so “123456789” also finds “123-456-789”. The matched text is deleted from the saved PDF, not just covered. Check the boxes before saving.
